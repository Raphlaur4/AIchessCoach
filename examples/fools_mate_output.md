## What happened in this game

This game lasted only **2 moves for White** — it's actually called the **Fool's Mate**, and it's the fastest possible checkmate in all of chess. The good news? It only happens by making two very specific mistakes in a row, so once you understand why, you'll never fall for it again.

---

### ⚠️ Move 1 — **f3** (Mistake)

**What went wrong:** White pushed the pawn in front of the king's knight. This pawn was acting as a shield for the king — moving it starts to remove that protection.

**Why it matters:** The squares around your king are like a wall. Every pawn you move from that area is a brick you're taking out of the wall.

**Remember:** Early in the game, focus on moving your **centre pawns** (e2 and d2) to control the middle of the board — not the pawns on the edges of your king's shelter.

---

### 🚨 Move 3 — **g4** (Blunder → Immediate Checkmate)

**What was lost:** Everything — the game. This move opened up a clear path (a *diagonal*) straight to White's king for Black's queen.

**Why it happened:** After f3 already weakened the king's side, pushing g4 removed the last pawn guarding the h4 square. Black's queen jumped to h4 and delivered **checkmate** — meaning the king was attacked with no way to escape.

**Remember:** Before moving a pawn near your king, ask yourself: *"Am I opening a line toward my own king?"* If yes, stop and think twice.

---

## The big lesson 🏰

**King safety comes first.** Your king lives in the corner and relies on the pawns in front of it to stay safe. Moving f3 and g4 is like opening the front door *and* the side door to your house at the same time. Keep those pawns home until your king is castled and safe!
